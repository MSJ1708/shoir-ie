# Shoir-IE Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 25 October 2026  
**Version:** 2026-10-21-v1

## Who operates Shoir-IE
Shoir-IE is a product name for an evolving industrial-engineering software platform. The operator's full legal name, official business address and registration details must be published before public commercial launch. Contact: shoirtheagent@gmail.com. This document is not a substitute for identifying the legally responsible controller.

## Information processed
Depending on use, Shoir-IE may process account identifiers and contact details; password hashes and session metadata; subscription and payment-reference information; uploaded workbooks, datasets, configuration, simulations, decisions and exported reports; support/diagnostic text; and technical logs needed for operations and security. Do not submit unnecessary personal information, credentials, or data you are not authorized to share.

## Purposes and systems
Information may be used to authenticate users, save and restore workspaces, enforce subscriptions and permissions, investigate faults, respond to support requests, operate requested features, and maintain security. The application may rely on Streamlit and its hosting environment, Supabase for database, storage and persistence, and Anthropic or other configured AI providers for AI-assisted features. These services may process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

## AI and industrial data
Only submit prompts and data suitable for processing by the configured provider. We do not promise that third-party providers never retain prompts or that every provider offers the same retention or training settings. Avoid sending confidential production data or personal information to AI features unless your organization has approved the transfer and the configured terms.

## Location and international processing
Provider infrastructure or support may occur outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Transfers may be subject to provider arrangements and applicable law. Before commercial launch, the operator must complete and document a PDPL data inventory, lawful basis, retention schedule, processor arrangements, cross-border transfer assessment, and required notices/consents for the actual deployment.

## Retention, security and rights
Information should be retained only as long as needed for the stated purpose, legal obligations, dispute resolution or security. Technical and organizational controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Requests to access, correct or delete personal data, or ask a question about processing, may be sent to shoirtheagent@gmail.com. Response and retention practices must be finalized for the legally responsible operator and actual deployment.

## Changes
This policy may change during beta. The current version and effective date should be displayed during registration. A software test or policy notice does not certify compliance with Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) or any other law.
