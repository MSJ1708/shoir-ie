# Shoir-IE engineering calculation and decision-support notice

Shoir-IE provides software-assisted industrial-engineering calculations, data preparation, screening, forecasting, simulation, optimization, reports and decision-support workflows. The availability, accuracy and scope of a method depend on the selected tool, its implemented version, input quality, configuration, assumptions, dependencies and validation status.

## Inputs and outputs

The user is responsible for checking input origin, completeness, time period, units, currency, data quality, measurement system, sample design, constraints and applicability to the actual process. Any demonstration values, synthetic datasets, example records, unverified assumptions, user-entered values or illustrative ROI must remain clearly labelled and must not be described as independently verified plant evidence.

Outputs are decision-support information, not an automatic operational command, safety certification, regulatory approval, or a guarantee of an outcome. A tool catalog listing or passing software test does not mean that every formula or domain assumption has been independently certified. Numerical output should be checked against an appropriate independent reference case and domain expert before consequential use.

## Limitations and human oversight

Methods may omit relevant site-specific constraints, uncertainty, correlations, measurement error, environmental conditions, worker differences, maintenance history, contractual terms or regulatory requirements. Simulation estimates depend on model structure and inputs; heuristic or approximate methods must not be described as proven global optima. Statistical screening is not necessarily a causal conclusion. ROI calculations do not prove realized savings.

Users must independently verify outputs and assumptions and obtain qualified engineering or other professional review appropriate to the decision. Do not use Shoir-IE alone to authorize machinery operation, bypass a safety control, determine whether a person is medically fit, approve a hazardous task, or make an irreversible safety-critical, employment, quality-release, maintenance or ergonomic decision.

No module should connect to live equipment or execute an external action unless that specific integration, permission boundary, validation, monitoring and rollback behavior is expressly implemented and approved. An unavailable, demo, session-only or unverified function should be labelled accurately.

## Traceability

Retain the input source, timestamp/time zone, units, formula or method version, assumptions, warnings, data provenance, tool ID, output, reviewer and decision record where supported. Confirm save status; a result visible in the current session is not proof of a durable save. Maintain independent backups where needed.

## Claims and responsibility

Do not describe simulated, synthetic, predicted, estimated or user-asserted benefits as realized or independently verified. Any savings claim must identify baseline and comparison period, data source, calculation method, confounders, uncertainty, and the authorized reviewer who independently confirmed the result.
